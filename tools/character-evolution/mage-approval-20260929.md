# Mages — approved scope, 29 September 2026

The authors approved the six Mage proposals after the Aseryn release (43477642).

Keep all six costs and all mastery/amplitude progression unchanged. Short cards: one or two sentences, with complete details retained. Preserve canonical IDs, all other Natures and all Fléau rules.

- Équilibrage du Flux: same first-switch reduction, floor zero.
- Décharge contrôlée: same 1 PA / difficulty 15 / once per round / -1 Tension, narrative failure +1.
- Ancrage du Mageius: add +3 to concentration against interruption; retain protection of the Mageius, no equivalent stacking, no Revers/Dormance benefit.
- Résonance héritée: explicitly show one-level intrinsic difficulty reduction and the Initiale/Mineure echo without awakened affinity.
- Œuvre personnelle: an identified signature technique with a specific exception and fixed parameters, existing dominant-affinity prerequisites retained.
- Héritage familial: identified, taught technique; check its actual affinity/mastery/amplitude requirements, not an arbitrary mastery purchase.

Implement structured persisted technique sheets for Echo, personal Work and family Heritage: name, effect, affinities, mastery/amplitude, PA, range, Tension and exception. Render on owner and shared/campaign sheets; keep original/campaign isolation and reward protections. Do not treat a player's text or acknowledgement as actual GM authorization. No invented Mage technique or unapproved Daemon balance changes.

This file records approval and captures current tracked source for isolated integration tests; it does not claim delivery.
